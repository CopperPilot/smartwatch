    - `U6` (`ETA7014S2G`, 36 V input overvoltage protection load switch in SOT-23-6, LCSC `C7465500`, `R_ON = 35 mOhm` typ) sits on `/VCHG_IN` (`U6.4`, with `VCC`/`EN` `U6.5`/`U6.2` filtered via `R29 = 1 kOhm` `0402WGF1001TCE`, LCSC `C11702` + `C23 = 1 uF` 50 V X5R 0402 `GRM155R61H105KE05D`, LCSC `C1518208`) and outputs `/VCHG_PROT` (`U6.3`, bypassed by `C26 = 1 uF` 25 V X5R 0402 `CL05A105KA5NQNC`, LCSC `C52923`). Precision `0.1%` thin-film divider `R27 = 35.7 kOhm` (`ERA-2AEB3572X`, LCSC `C2075580`) and `R28 = 10.0 kOhm` (`RT0402BRD0710KL`, LCSC `C190095`) on `OVP_FB` (`U6.1`, `V_FB = 1.26 V +/- 0.05 V` = `1.21 V` min to `1.31 V` max) programs the overvoltage cutoff threshold to `V_OVP(typ) = 1.26 V * (35.7 + 10.0) / 10.0 = 5.758 V`, with worst-case minimum `V_OVP(min) = 1.21 V * (35.7 * 0.999 + 10.0 * 1.001) / (10.0 * 1.001) = 5.521 V > 5.500 V` (guaranteeing normal `4.75–5.50 V` USB `vSafe5V` input never trips OVP) and worst-case maximum `V_OVP(max) = 1.31 V * (35.7 * 1.001 + 10.0 * 0.999) / (10.0 * 0.999) = 5.996 V < 6.000 V` (strictly below the `6.5 V` recommended operating and `7.0 V` Absolute Maximum Rating of downstream `U8` `TPS2553DBVR`, and below the `6.0 V` recommended operating and `7.0 V` Absolute Maximum Rating of `U2` `MCP73832T-2ACI/OT`), while `U6`'s internal soft-start controls capacitive hot-plug inrush into `C26` (`1 uF`) and `C11` (`4.7 uF`).

## 2. Display Backlight Constant-Current Driver, UI Protection & Sensor Level Shifting

- **Display FPC (`J1`, `FPC-05FB-12PH20`, LCSC `C2856829`) & Constant-Current Backlight Driver (`U7`, `CAT4002ASD-GT3`, LCSC `C890382`):**
  - 12-pin 0.5 mm pitch double-sided contact FPC connector `J1` mates with the 12-pin 1.9-inch `170x320` `ST7789V2` IPS TFT module (`ZJY190S0800TG01` / `GMT169-01`), with `100 kOhm` pull-ups `R39`/`R40` on `LCD_CS`/`LCD_RES`, separate `LEDA` (Pin 3, tied to `/VSYS` with `1 uF` bypass `C25`), and `LEDK` (Pin 2, `/LCD_LEDK`) regulated by `U7` (`CAT4002ASD-GT3`, `R14 = 3.6 kOhm` for `41.7 mA` total sink current, dimmed via 1-wire EZDim pulse bursts on `U7.1` from `U1.25`).
- **Sealed-Enclosure Long-Press Push-Button Hardware Reset Supervisor (`U9`, `TPS3421EGDRYR`, LCSC `C2067262`):**
  - `U9` (`TPS3421EGDRYR`, `250 nA` push-button reset controller in SON-6, `TS` tied to `/GND` for `7.5 s` setup delay) monitors `SW2` (`/BTN_SIDE_SW`) and asserts a `400 ms` active-low hardware reset pulse on `/MCU_EN` (`U1.45`) when `SW2` is held for `>= 7.5 s`.
- **Rotary Encoder (`SW1`, `EC05E1220203`) & Tactile Button (`SW2`/`SW3`, `SKRTLAE010`) Contact-Current & ESD Protection:**
  - Switch terminals have `10 kOhm` pull-ups `R3..R6` to `+3V3` and 5 V bidirectional ESD TVS diodes `D5..D8` (`PESD5V0S1BL`, LCSC `C2912556`), followed by `10 kOhm` series current-limiting/ESD-isolation resistors `R30..R33` feeding `10 nF` encoder filter capacitors `C17..C18` (`<= 0.33 mA` contact discharge current) and `100 nF` tactile switch debounce capacitors `C19..C20`.
- **1.8 V <-> 3.3 V Fast-Mode I2C & Interrupt Level Translation (`Q3..Q5`, `BSS138K`, LCSC `C5224263`):**
  - `Q3`, `Q4`, and `Q5` use low-capacitance sub-1V-threshold small-signal N-MOSFETs (`BSS138K`, `C_iss = 25 pF`, `C_oss = 9.7 pF`, `C_rss = 2.2 pF`, `V_GS(th) = 0.4–1.0 V`) with gates on `+1V8`, `1.8 kOhm` pull-ups `R7, R8` (to `+1V8`) and `R20, R21` (to `+3V3`, `0402WGF1801TCE`, LCSC `C25871`) on `I2C_SCL` and `I2C_SDA` (yielding RC rise time `t_r ~ 70 ns << 300 ns` 400 kHz Fast-Mode limit while keeping total low-level sink current `I_OL = 2.83 mA < 3.0 mA`), and `10 kOhm` pull-ups `R9` (to `+1V8`) and `R22` (to `+3V3`, `0402WGF1002TCE`, LCSC `C25744`) on open-drain interrupt `HR_INT`.
