Build a rugged smartwatch like the one in these photos. It should fit the same standard watch bands, have
the same buttons, crown and display size, and track heart rate.

Decisions already made:
- Bands: a bought standard 49 mm sport-style band (model it from the listing drawing, without logos); our case
  provides lug channels that fit its connector bars, and the band slides sideways into them.
- Charging like the reference: from the back (charging contacts for a bought magnetic charging cable). No port
  or opening on the sides other than what the photos show.
- Crown: a small side-mounted rotary encoder whose shaft points out through a sealed bore in the case wall; the
  crown is a cap pressed onto that shaft. The side button and the action button press side-actuated switches at
  the board edge through plungers.
- Display: the same size as the reference, about 1.9 inch diagonal. The flat cover glass covers almost the whole
  front inside a thin raised metal rim (about 1.5-2 mm of case showing), with a printed black border round the
  active area. It is bonded onto a ledge in the case (adhesive).
- Buttons: the side button and the action button are pushed in from outside and clip in (snap fingers or a bought
  E-clip), so they cannot be pulled back out. The crown cap presses onto the encoder shaft through a sealed bore.
- Profile: match the reference's side profile, with a lower case side and a domed sensor back rather than a
  straight-sided box. Use low-profile parts so the real stack fits.
