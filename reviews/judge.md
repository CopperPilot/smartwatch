# Independent judge

A separate agent compares the renders of the finished design with the reference photos, without seeing the design files or the other agents' notes.

**Overall 8/10** · form 8 · features 9 · requirements 9 (pass mark 7).

## Differences from the reference

- The transverse band lug channels are cut into flat vertical top and bottom end walls rather than blending into angled underside lug chamfers as on the reference case.
- The active window of the off-the-shelf 1.9-inch 170x320 rectangular TFT panel leaves a slightly wider lateral black mask border under the cover glass than the reference custom squircle display.
- The rear sensor dome incorporates a dedicated 2-pin magnetic pogo contact cutout next to the central optical sensor window as specified for the magnetic pogo charging cable, and the left speaker grille uses an 8-hole two-row array adjacent to the action button rather than the reference 10-hole three-row pattern.
